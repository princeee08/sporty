VOLLEYBALL

Volleyball is a team sport played between two teams separated by a net. The main objective is to send the ball over the net and make it land on the opponent’s court while preventing the other team from doing the same. A team normally has six players on the court during indoor volleyball. Volleyball involves skills such as serving, passing, setting, attacking, blocking, and digging. Players must work together to keep the ball from touching the ground on their side of the court. Communication and teamwork are important because players must quickly react to the movement of the ball.

HISTORY

Volleyball was invented by William G. Morgan in 1895 in Massachusetts, USA. The original game was called Mintonette and was designed as an indoor activity. The name was later changed to volleyball because players repeatedly volleyed the ball over the net. The sport spread to different countries and became internationally organized with the establishment of the FIVB in 1947. Volleyball became an official Olympic sport in 1964, helping increase its popularity around the world.

BENEFITS

  • Improves physical fitness: Volleyball keeps the body active through running, jumping, and movement.
  • Builds strength: Jumping, hitting, and blocking help strengthen muscles.
  • Improves coordination: Passing, setting, and hitting develop hand-eye coordination.
  • Improves agility: Players need to move quickly around the court.
  • Improves balance: Jumping and landing help develop body control.
